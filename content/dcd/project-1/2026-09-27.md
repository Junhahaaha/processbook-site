4. 문제 상황 페이지 2 (동일 목차)
오늘날 우리 사회의 교육 격차 문제는 날이 갈수록 심화되고 있습니다.
소득에 따라 교육에 투자되는 비용은 큰 격차를 보입니다.
2024년 초중고사교육비조사에 따르면, 월소득 800만원 이상 가구는 일반교과에만 월평균 67만 6천원을 쓰는 반면, 300만원 미만 가구는 20만 5천원에 그쳐 3배 이상의 격차가 존재합니다.
교육 과정에 따라서 선택 과목으로 분류되는 에체능은 그중에서도 먼저 줄어드는 경향을 보입니다.
특히 예체능에 대한 우리 사회의 인식은 흔히, 돈이 많이 드는 과목, 전문적인 교육이 요구되는 과목으로 인식되곤 합니다.
이에 학생들은, 해당 분야에 관심이 있더라도 공부할 수 있는 충분한 기회를 제공받지 못하는 문제가 발생합니다.

**3. Social Issue (1/2)**  
The issue this project addresses is SDG 4 — reducing educational inequality. Within that, we focused specifically on arts education, and music education in particular.

**4. Social Issue (2/2)**  
The education gap in our society is deepening by the day. Spending on education varies sharply by household income.

_[Income-gap chart]_

Arts education, often classified as an optional subject in the curriculum, tends to be the first thing cut when budgets are tight. Our society commonly perceives arts education as "expensive" and "requiring specialized instruction." As a result, students who are interested don't always get a real chance to learn.

**5. Persona**  
We captured this problem through a persona.

**Kim Midi, First-Year High School Student**

He's become interested in composition and music and wants to start learning, but the burden of academy fees keeps him from bringing it up with his parents. He thinks, "If only I had the equipment and some basic instruction."

**6. Project Introduction (1/2)**  
MOSAIC is the project built to solve this problem. Through open-source resources and free education online, students can use music equipment at low cost and receive music education through it.

**7. Project Introduction (2/2)**  
The project consists of building modular, expandable music equipment and learning music theory with AI. Through this process, students can build their own equipment without financial burden and learn the basics of music theory for free. We aim to deliver this whole learning journey through a media experience.

**8. Experience Design (1/4)**  
The project runs through two media: video and a website.

**9. Experience Design (2/4)**  
The first medium is video — a campaign video that conveys MOSAIC's core values and content, motivating students to learn and sparking interest in the project.

**10. Experience Design (3/4)**  
The second medium is the website — the space where the MOSAIC project actually happens, where students build open-source hardware and receive AI-driven, personalized instruction.

**11. Experience Design (4/4)**  
Together, these two media deliver a technology-driven, active learning experience: the first sparks interest and motivation, the second is where that gets realized.

**12. Design Style (1/3)**  
For video, we're planning usage footage centered on the open-source hardware — in a format similar to a product introduction video, covering the flow from making to using, alongside an introduction to the campaign.

**13. Design Style (2/3)**  
For the website, we plan an interactive site built with 3D assets — using a semi-realistic graphic style like tumbler/merch brand websites, so that selecting, assembling, and expanding the product feels easy even for students.

**14. Design Style (3/3)**  
For education, we use an AI-powered chatbot system and visualization-based instruction for music theory — enabling active learning rather than simple one-way instruction.

**15. Expected Outcomes**  
Choosing video — the medium students find easiest to engage with — lowers resistance and increases accessibility. For web design, an interactive 3D-based guide (rather than a plain graphic guide) makes the process easier to follow. AI-driven instruction also lets users find learning enjoyable. Together, these can ease the existing, burdensome perception of music education.

**16. Closing**  
Through this project, we aim to realize "music education open to everyone." Thank you.
